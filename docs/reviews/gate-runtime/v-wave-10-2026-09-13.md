## PROPOSED LESSONS

**Index line:**
`- [two-sided exemption deletion](two-sided-exemption-deletion-owes-the-phantom-drive.md) — a gate's stale-exemption arm saying a row is dead is not evidence the cite is gone`

**Body:**

> Deleting a row from a two-sided exemption table because the gate's own STALE arm reported it owes a
> re-drive of the PHANTOM arm on the same tree before the commit lands, not just after.
>
> **Why:** `1d83cf962` deleted `dangling-refs`' `ARM3_ALLOW` `"domain/hub"` row citing the gate's own
> unreferenced-exemption arm, and left two live `domain/hub` phantom cites at
> `docs/architecture/core/Core-Path-Registry.md:146` reporting on every run since. Both arms live in one
> gate and answered opposite things about one subject; the lane read one and shipped. The commit's floor
> line claimed "1 -> 0" and the tree says 2.
>
> **How to apply:** when a diff removes an exemption row, the receipt is a full-gate drive AFTER the removal
> showing the finding count unchanged — never the stale arm's report, which is a claim about the table and
> not about the corpus. The cheap control in the other direction is a byte-copy SIBLING scratch module with
> only that row restored (`§4.1`'s patched-sibling idiom): if restoring it changes the count, the row was
> load-bearing and the deletion was wrong.

**Index line:**
`- [reason names a field, not just a symbol](public-reason-names-the-reachable-field-not-only-the-type.md) — #2228's "by SYMBOL" rule leaves the FIELD coordinate unchecked`

**Body:**

> A `@public knip type-face` reason of the house form "a structural field (`f`) of the exported `T` shape" is
> TWO claims, and stripping the `(line N)` coordinate only fixes the third one. `T` existing is not enough:
> `f` must actually be the field whose TYPE is the annotated declaration.
>
> **Why:** three reasons in `tooling/src/verify/contract/resource-installed.ts` say each of
> `InstalledPackageMetadata`/`Text`/`Declarations` is the type of `InstalledPackageFacts`'s `id` field. It
> is not — `id` is an `InstalledPackageId`, and the three are intersection members of the union's three
> `mode` arms. Sampling ten reasons after the #2228 strip found three false in one file.
>
> **How to apply:** when auditing these reasons, read the named type's declaration and check that the named
> field's annotation IS the subject. An intersection member, a union arm and a field are three different
> reachability shapes and the reason should say which. `bus-definition-fact.ts:34` + `:49` is the correct
> pattern to compare against.
