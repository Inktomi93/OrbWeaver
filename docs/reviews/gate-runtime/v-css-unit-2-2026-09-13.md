## PROPOSED LESSONS

*(index entries + bodies; the orchestrator owns the memory write — I did not touch the store.)*

**Index:** `- [transplanted control loses its cut](transplanted-control-loses-its-cut.md) — a verifier's planted control committed as a row after the fixture it stood on was shrunk in the same commit`

> **A PLANTED CONTROL COMMITTED AS A PROOF ROW MUST BE RE-MEASURED AGAINST THE FIXTURE IT LANDS ON.** A
> verifier's control is a DELTA against a fixture ("a THIRD carrier"), not an absolute shape. When the fixing
> commit also SHRINKS that fixture — which is exactly what a "the counts are gone, so the fixtures can shrink"
> repair does — the transplanted row is a different delta and can be silently inert against the very thing it
> is advertised to stop. Worked case 2026-09-13: `css-family-ownership-health` `mustPass[5]` shipped as "a
> THIRD `:root` blur carrier" while `BLUR_SEAM_COMPLETE` went 2 carriers → 1 in the same commit; the committed
> row is a SECOND carrier at exactly `CLIENT_BLUR_FILL.size * 2` declarations, i.e. silent under the retired
> cardinality its `why` says it reds. **How to apply:** when landing a verifier's control as a row, re-run the
> control's own arithmetic against the fixture as committed, and say the number.

**Index:** `- [cut the LAST member of the set](cut-every-member-of-a-seam-set-not-two-of-three.md) — a per-member sweep that stops one short reads as a proven family`

> **A PER-MEMBER §4.1 SWEEP MUST CUT EVERY MEMBER, BECAUSE THE UNCUT ONE IS WHERE THE MISSING ROW IS.** A
> table-driven policy (three seams, six kinds, N registry rows) invites a sweep that cuts a representative
> two and generalises. Measured 2026-09-13: `css-family-policy.ts`'s three runtime-writer seams were swept as
> b04 (blur) and b05 (density) — both enforced — and the commit generalised to "emptying a seam's member set
> kills its row"; cutting the third (colorization) killed ZERO, because it has no `mustFlag` row at all. The
> arm was proven REACHED (adding a bogus member reds 10 rows) and proven UNPINNED in the direction that
> matters. **How to apply:** enumerate the table's keys, cut each, and report the matrix — the row you skipped
> is the one with no proof.

**Index:** `- [pins can land 18 minutes before the prose that denies them](citation-staleness-is-minutes-not-days.md) — re-derive a sibling gate's enforcement state at WRITE time, not from the review you read`

> **A CITATION TO ANOTHER GATE'S UNENFORCED ARM GOES STALE IN MINUTES ON A FIVE-LANE TRAIN, NOT IN DAYS.**
> `css-selector-has-a-writer-health`'s header shipped "those directions are measured unenforced by any proof
> row under OPEN #2297" in a commit whose own ancestor, 18 minutes older, had pinned all three. The lane read
> the verifier report (correct when written) instead of the tree. **How to apply:** before writing "X is
> unenforced under open #N", `git log --oneline -3 -- <X's module>` and read its `mustFlag` array. And prefer
> citing the ISSUE (which stays open for its other rows) over restating the measurement.
