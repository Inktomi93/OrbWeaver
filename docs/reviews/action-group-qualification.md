## Remaining acceptance and limits

The Preset header passes native containment, compact grouping and actual failed-save/Retry cases. The [Preset image review](../../reports/side-eye/2026-10-27-preset-params-comparison.md) accepts actual phone, short-landscape and desktop presentation while retaining identity, feedback, actions and tabs.

The separate tab-reveal P2 belongs to the broader visual goal, which remains open. This action-group result does not qualify automatic reveal or every tab journey.

The [collection image review](../../reports/side-eye/2026-10-27-collection-inline-create-after.md) accepts empty/disabled and named/enabled phone presentation. Native cases independently establish narrow/enlarged containment, keyboard behavior and actual submit/cancel semantics. They do not submit the private review clone.

Fact resting actions and their revealed editor share a real max-output transition case. Other fact-input kinds remain outside that case's claim.

The maintained suites own their regimes, fixtures and assertions. Native teardown warnings remain visible; passing summaries do not imply warning-free runs.

No full mutation, authorization, physical-device, real Safari, keyboard/OS integration or whole-application visual acceptance follows from these component cases. Main owns final visual disposition and work status.
