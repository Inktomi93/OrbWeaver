# Cross-surface comparison and repair

## Acceptance

Compare related tasks at matching appearance settings and effective content widths. Legal tokens and isolated containment checks do not establish visual consistency.

Use production defaults as the reference configuration, not as an approved screenshot baseline. Record appearance, pointer, viewport, panel state and data state with each comparison.

Keep stress configurations separate from the reference comparison. Check affected layouts with enlarged text, coarse portrait, short landscape and applicable panel combinations.

An accepted family needs coherent hierarchy, proportions, alignment, action placement and reading flow. Preserve task-specific differences with an explicit reason.

Fix confirmed shared defects and affected adoption before accepting the family. Retain failures and unvisited states without treating them as passes.

## Verification scheduling

- Freeze all checkout writes before native runners. The working-tree guard also applies to Node tests.

- Separate fixture errors, instrument failures and demonstrated product failures.
- Reuse passing evidence while its source, fixture and execution scope remain unchanged.
- Complete independent source review and comparative rendered review for each repaired family.
- Keep the broader goal active until the coverage families and applicable action-group candidates have explicit final dispositions.
