  any caller signal (`AbortSignal.any`). **Bound argued: 120s** — matching the agent-sdk summarize watchdog

  promise SETTLES (→ the rpg flush promise settles → the barrier `.finally` clears the entry). No raw
  DOMException leaks.
- Pin: `tests/.../vllm/engine/client.test.ts` — (a) `enginePost` ALWAYS fetches with an abort signal
  (never undefined); (b) a request-timeout abort → retryable server ProviderError (the flush settles,
