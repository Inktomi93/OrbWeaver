# A declared knob is wired or cited as dormant

## Context

Not recorded in the ledger row.

## Decision

**A declared knob is WIRED or CITED-DORMANT; DOORWAY vs DEFERRED is the dormant-vs-dead distinction.** A settings field/section/leaf, an `AppSettings` admin-editor key, a `DEFAULT_FORMAT_STRINGS` format string, or a `chatMetadataSchema` field that validates + stores but is never WRITTEN or never READ is a **dead switch** — an edit silently governs nothing (the `rateLimits` dead-ended-pair, the permanently-OFF `memory.enabled`, the read-by-nobody `dupThreshold`). The `knob-wire-coverage` gate makes it structural: every declared knob has its missing half wired, OR carries a cited entry in ONE of two module-const registries, each self-cleaning in BOTH directions (the D50 bus-coverage ratchet discipline — a member that GAINS its wire REDs its stale entry; a member that LOSES it REDs missing). The two registries encode the owner's dormant-vs-dead ruling as first-class state: **DOORWAY** = a SANCTIONED, indefinitely-dormant rebuild seam (a declared doorway a purged/future domain will graft onto — never re-litigated, only stale-checked); **DEFERRED** = TRACKED DEBT with a remediation cite (a knob that SHOULD be wired and isn't yet). The canonical "declared doorway later wired" success story the DOORWAY state exists to protect is `workloads.dependsOn`/`scheduledAt` (declared unwired, since fully wired: client run-dialog + tRPC + the scheduler DAG gate) — DOORWAY is what lets that pattern ship without an orphan-export gate fighting "unwired ≠ worthless" (Core-0 §1). **The databank settings machinery is WIRED (Phase B ④ — was a sanctioned dormant doorway by owner ruling, commit `09cd25525`):** `UserSettings.databank` (`{chunk, retrieval:{k,minScore,rerank}, slotTokenBudget}`, schema v5) now exists with a client write path (a settings-section contribution at the chat-behavior anchor); the compose `getDatabankSettings(ownerId)` reads the user's real section via `loadUserSettings` (the 0-param schema-default stub is gone — it fed ingest chunk params too); and the retrieval params ride ForeignInputs → `gatherRetrieval` → `search.documents` (`k`/`minScore`/`rerank` consumed field-for-field, defaults byte-identical to pre-wire — reuse-seam-check-both-ends verified).
