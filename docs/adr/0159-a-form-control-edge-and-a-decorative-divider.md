# a FORM-CONTROL edge and a DECORATIVE divider are two tokens, not one: `--color-input-border` holds WCAG 1.4.11's 3:1, `--color-border` deliberately does not

## Context

Not recorded in the ledger row.

## Decision

The orchestrator reconciled two owner texts from the same batch: an earlier text — *"raise `--color-border` in the vault and hold 3:1 for form-control borders … re-tune the token across all three seeds"*; a later text in the same batch — *"mint a NEW polarity-aware `input-border` token on the `--color-selection-quiet` precedent … the 72-consumer `--color-border` STAYS"*. The ruling kept the later text's outcome: the mint. **The ruling survives; its INPUT changed.** What stands from the earlier text: the symptom (45 `border-contrast` findings on settings:chat-behavior `--mobile`, 5 on appearance, 8 on admin), the 3:1 form-control floor, the palette-contrast pin, the framebuffer CT, and the design-audit-reports-0-for-the-class result. What is replaced: the retune step, by the minted token on every form-control carrier. **The evidence that decided it:** `color.border` is `oklch(0.99 0.005 60 / 0.08)` — an 8%-alpha DIVIDER hairline with ~72 consumers, and 1.4.11 governs the boundary of a user-interface component while saying nothing about a decorative rule; `color.input` at alpha 0.12 is the control FILL, not its edge; and every failing site (select-trigger, textarea, input-group) is a FORM CONTROL. Raising all 72 consumers to satisfy a floor binding on a dozen is the shouting-divider regression the mint avoids. **The vocabulary split, which is the durable half:** `--color-input-border` is the edge of anything a user OPERATES (input · textarea · select trigger · combobox · autocomplete · number-field group and its steppers · checkbox · radio · switch track · colour swatch · file dropzone — the `FIELD_CONTROL_BOX` / `SELECTION_CONTROL` fragments are its two shared homes, `packages/ui/src/lib/`); `--color-border` is every non-control edge (card, table, accordion, tabs track, separators, popover chrome) and stays a quiet hairline on purpose. A form-control edge wearing `border-border` is the defect this row names. **Bounded from BOTH sides** (the \[\[D144]] measured-value posture, one token over): FLOOR 3:1 against every panel AND the `bg-input` fill composited over it, worst of the float and 8-bit-quantized ratio — 3.257 (Hearth) / 3.369 (Light) / 3.257 (Mocha), worst ground the `bg-accent` an interactive row paints on hover; CEILING under the FOCUS RING on `--color-card` — 6.22 vs 6.82 / 5.67 vs 6.27 / 6.14 vs 6.65 — so a resting edge never outshouts the focused state. Rendered evidence, `pixelExtremaContrast` on the empty control across three seeds × three panel fills: 1.699–1.912:1 before, 5.217–6.824:1 after, borderless positive control 1.000:1 in both runs. **The appearance settings do NOT reach form-control edges** and this row is the answer to that question so it is not re-asked: `appearance.elevation` (flat|ramp|glow) writes `.shell-grid[data-elevation]` panel chrome only — `ramp` deletes panel/band/rail hairlines and lifts panel FILLS to `--color-surface-raised`/`--color-card`, `glow` adds `--shadow-overlay` — frosted glass blurs panel backdrops, and the `prefers-contrast: more` block raises panel EDGES while deliberately leaving card borders alone; none of them writes an `input-root` / select-trigger / textarea edge. Because what `ramp` changes is WHICH panel token a surface paints, sweeping the panel set IS the sweep over every elevation mode, and the floor holds on all of them. **The token is STATIC, not ThemeScope-emitted, and the asymmetry with its sibling is the point:** a decorative divider owes no ratio and re-derives per custom base, while this one carries a two-sided MEASURED bound a derivation cannot hold — so it is ONE `orb.output: light-dark` token whose active side follows `color-scheme` — the \[\[D71]] pipeline's static-side shape, and exactly the `--color-selection-quiet` precedent (it is NOT one of D71 clause (3)'s INTENT colors, which is why that clause's enumeration stays complete), and one dark side serves both dark seeds (Mocha clears on the Hearth side at 3.257, so there is no mocha value-set row). Homes: `packages/ui/src/tokens/tokens.json` `color.input-border` (its `$description` carries the per-seed numbers) · `packages/ui/src/tokens/themes/light.json` (the light side) · `packages/ui/src/lib/field-control.ts` + `packages/ui/src/lib/selection-control.ts` (the carriers). Enforcers, all three live: the two-sided pin in `tests/ui/content/theme-scope/palette-contrast.suite.test.ts` (floor + ceiling per seed, plus the row asserting `--color-border` is still BELOW 3:1 — the evidence that decided this split, kept as a test so a later retune cannot leave two tokens quietly doing one job); the framebuffer case in `tests/ui/primitives/input/input.ct.tsx` (three seeds × three panel fills, with a borderless positive control in the same mount); and the `border-contrast` class of `design-audit`, which reports 0 for form controls. Its static-not-emitted classification is registered in `tests/ui/content/theme-scope/token-classification.suite.test.ts` and `tests/ui/tokens/theme-emit-pairing.suite.test.ts`.
