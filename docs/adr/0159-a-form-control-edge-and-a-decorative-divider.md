# a FORM-CONTROL edge and a DECORATIVE divider are two tokens, not one: `--color-input-border` holds WCAG 1.4.11's 3:1, `--color-border` deliberately does not

## Context

Not recorded in the ledger row.

## Decision

The orchestrator reconciled two owner texts from the same batch: an earlier text — *"raise `--color-border` in the vault and hold 3:1 for form-control borders … re-tune the token across all three seeds"*; a later text in the same batch — *"mint a NEW polarity-aware `input-border` token on the `--color-selection-quiet` precedent … the 72-consumer `--color-border` STAYS"*. The ruling kept the later text's outcome: the mint. **The ruling survives; its INPUT changed.** What stands from the earlier text: the symptom (45 `border-contrast` findings on settings:chat-behavior `--mobile`, 5 on appearance, 8 on admin), the 3:1 form-control floor, the palette-contrast pin, the framebuffer CT, and the design-audit-reports-0-for-the-class result. What is replaced: the retune step, by the minted token on every form-control carrier. **The evidence that decided it:** `color.border` is `oklch(0.99 0.005 60 / 0.08)` — an 8%-alpha DIVIDER hairline with ~72 consumers, and 1.4.11 governs the boundary of a user-interface component while saying nothing about a decorative rule; `color.input` at alpha 0.12 is the control FILL, not its edge; and every failing site (select-trigger, textarea, input-group) is a FORM CONTROL. Raising all 72 consumers to satisfy a floor binding on a dozen is the shouting-divider regression the mint avoids. **The vocabulary split, which is the durable half:** `--color-input-border` is the edge of anything a user OPERATES (input · textarea · select trigger · combobox · autocomplete · number-field group and its steppers · checkbox · radio · switch track · colour swatch · file dropzone — the `FIELD_CONTROL_BOX` / `SELECTION_CONTROL` fragments are its two shared homes, `packages/ui/src/lib/`); `--color-border` is every non-control edge (card, table, accordion, tabs track, separators, popover chrome) and stays a quiet hairline on purpose. A form-control edge wearing `border-border` is the defect this row names. **Bounded from BOTH sides** (the \[\[D144]] measured-value posture, one token over): FLOOR 3:1 against every panel AND the `bg-input` fill composited over it, worst of the float and 8-bit-quantized ratio — 3.257 (Hearth) / 3.369 (Light) / 3.257 (Mocha), worst ground the `bg-accent` an interactive row paints on hover; CEILING under the FOCUS RING on `--color-card` — 6.22 vs 6.82 / 5.67 vs 6.27 / 6.14 vs 6.65 — so a resting edge never outshouts the focused state. Rendered evidence, `pixelExtremaContrast` on the empty control across three seeds × three panel fills: 1.699–1.912:1 before, 5.217–6.824:1 after, borderless positive control 1.000:1 in both runs. **The appearance settings do NOT reach form-control edges** and this row is the answer to that question so it is not re-asked: `appearance.elevation` (flat|ramp|glow) writes `.shell-grid[data-elevation]` panel chrome only — `ramp` deletes panel/band/rail hairlines and lifts panel FILLS to `--color-surface-raised`/`--color-card`, `glow` adds `--shadow-overlay` — frosted glass blurs panel backdrops, and the `prefers-contrast: more` block raises panel EDGES while deliberately leaving card borders alone; none of them writes an `input-root` / select-trigger / textarea edge. Because what `ramp` changes is WHICH panel token a surface paints, sweeping the panel set IS the sweep over every elevation mode, and the floor holds on all of them. **The token is static on the seeds and emitted under a carried background.** A decorative divider owes no ratio and re-derives per custom base. This one carries a two-sided MEASURED bound, so on the seeds it is ONE `orb.output: light-dark` token whose active side follows `color-scheme` (the \[\[D71]] static-side shape and the `--color-selection-quiet` precedent; it is NOT one of D71 clause (3)'s INTENT colors). One dark side serves both dark seeds (Mocha clears on the Hearth side at 3.257), so there is no mocha value-set row. A fixed pair cannot hold the floor over a background the user picked: on a carried `oklch(0.6 0 0)` the seed edge measured 1.46:1 against its host. So a ThemeScope that carries a background emits `--color-input-border`, solved over the scope's own panels the way the \[\[D336]] knobs are: the nearest lightness to the base, on either side, that holds 3:1 against the background, raised, card, popover, sidebar, accent, secondary and muted panels and against the `bg-input` fill composited over each (8-bit pixels), and the scope's focus ring must stay above it on the card. Focus out-shouts rest, so the ceiling is held by raising the ring, not by loosening the floor: when the solved edge does not sit below the accent fill on the card, the scope emits `--color-ring` as the accent's own hue at the nearest lightness whose ratio on the card is at least 3:1 and above the edge's by a fixed margin (`solveFocusRing`). `--color-primary` is not moved. Only when no lightness on that hue can clear the edge does the scope report the edge it painted (`ClampedTheme.inputBorderFallback`, delivered to `InputBorderFallbackReporterContext`, which the app shell provides beside the tone reporter). A scope with no known accent has no ring to judge, so only the floor applies. The carriers stay on `border-input-border`; no primitive overrides the token. Homes: `packages/ui/src/tokens/tokens.json` `color.input-border` (its `$description` carries the per-seed numbers) · `packages/ui/src/tokens/themes/light.json` (the light side) · `packages/ui/src/lib/field-control.ts` + `packages/ui/src/lib/selection-control.ts` (the carriers) · `packages/ui/src/content/theme-scope/derive-vars.ts` (`inputBorderOn`, the carried-scope emit) · `@orb/kit/theme-derivation` (`solveInputBorder` and `solveFocusRing`, the numeric solves) · `packages/ui/src/content/theme-scope/emit-vars.ts` (`THEME_SCOPE_EMIT_VARS` and `THEME_SCOPE_STATIC_SEED_VARS`). Enforcers, all three live: the two-sided pin in the palette-contrast suite (floor + ceiling per seed, plus the row asserting `--color-border` is still BELOW 3:1 — the evidence that decided this split, kept as a test so a later retune cannot leave two tokens quietly doing one job); the framebuffer case in the input CT (three seeds × three panel fills, with a borderless positive control in the same mount); and the `border-contrast` class of `design-audit`, which reports 0 for form controls. Its classification is registered in `THEME_SCOPE_EMIT_VARS` (a carried scope emits it) and `THEME_SCOPE_STATIC_SEED_VARS` (its seed form stays the static polarity token), which `packages/ui/tokens.build.ts` pairs against the seed value-set contract. A unit suite pins the solve and the raised ring over the accepted-base matrix (`tests/ui/content/theme-scope/input-border.test.ts`) a carrier CT pins checkbox, radio, switch and input under the seeds and a custom scope with a strong and with an inherited accent, and a delivery CT pins both fallback reporters (`tests/ui/content/theme-scope/fallback-delivery.ct.tsx`) (`tests/ui/primitives/checkbox/platform-checkbox-contrast.suite.ct.tsx`). The palette-contrast, input, token-classification and theme-emit-pairing proofs are [restoration register](../plans/restoration-register/design.md) entries.
