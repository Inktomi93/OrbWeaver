## Decision

Turn shaping is a CAPABILITY AXIS (`ModelCapability.turns` — prefill/cache/mid-conv-system/roleHandling/squash), derived per wire-shape by `deriveWireShape` + curated refinement, NEVER guessed at a callsite.
