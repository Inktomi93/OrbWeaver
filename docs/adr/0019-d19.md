## Decision

Turn identity has three distinct concepts; there is no `callerUserId` term. `Principal.userId` is the authenticated caller of this request. `triggeredBy` is the initiating human — the caller on a direct send and the chain starter for auto/automation/plugin turns — and owns attribution and abort. `funderUserId` is the room host seat frozen at the turn boundary; that host's connection and credentials fund the whole turn. `runAsUserId` is the same frozen host and scopes assembly and tools: cards, books, preset, prose, settings, and tool authority. A host handoff transfers liability for subsequent turns; an already-deferred turn replays with its persisted frozen host. Homes: `domain/chat/engine/turn-identity.ts` · `domain/chat/verbs/turn.ts`.
