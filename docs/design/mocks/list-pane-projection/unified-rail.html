ARM C — JUDGED, NOT RECOMMENDED

What the unified rail buys, and what it costs

Buys: one mental model (every start-or-resume in one pane), ST-launcher familiarity, one fewer rail decision. Rendered above at its best — nesting by face with an "everything else" bucket.

  • The chats-as-rooms door. Blank chats, multi-human rooms, and the war-table style ensemble have no face to live under — the "Everything else" bucket is the two-list problem reborn inside one pane, with worse geography.
  • Nesting duplicates or elects. The Crimson Court renders under Azarael AND Sera (dashed red above) — or you elect a "primary" face per room, which is marinara's chats.characterIds ownership smell re-derived as UI. The grammar teaches chats-belong-to-characters even while the data stays clean (the D18 rider's exact warning, and the one cost no gate can catch).
  • The library's management jobs lose their home (bulk mode, tags, categorized view, import) — they are not launcher content and would need a second surface.
  • Structural churn: SECTION_IDS surgery, mobile tab re-curation, ⌘K, selection memory, gate fixtures — all for a launcher the LANDING surface already provides at CONTENT tier.
ARM B — RECOMMENDED SYNTHESIS

The chats pane learns faces

  • A compact faces strip (resume-recency order, cap ~8) over the LANDED filter-chip seam — tap Azarael and the pane is instantly her threads: chip, scoped rows, empty state and New-chat all already built. Clearing the chip is one visible ✕.
  • The grammar stays honest: "Filtered: Azarael" is visibly a FILTER you applied, not a folder she owns. Rooms render once. No rail surgery, no registry churn.
  • Together with Arm A this IS "the two slots combined": the character screen gets her history beside her face; the chats screen gets faces over its threads — and the rail keeps its shape.
