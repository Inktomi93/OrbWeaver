## Verdict

**REDRAW the CONTENT and CONTEXT boards; the LIST door and the drill contract stand.** The move itself is right and small on the tree: the band becomes a door (`selectConfigGroup` already does that half), the library host already exists (`config-collection-landing.tsx`, keyed by the #1203 law), world-info already drills in CONTENT behind a Back, and the phone needs one predicate flipped plus one rung added to `clear()`. But what the mock draws INSIDE CONTENT and CONTEXT is not a build target yet: three of its five stated decisions collide with recorded rulings (the regex on-row switch against an orchestrator-ruled "do not restore without re-opening" fork; Delete in editor headers against D121(D) and #271; tag drag-handles against the rows' own "a handle in a derived order is a control that lies"), two of its rows draw capabilities that have no verb (a regex library order; a bulk Export), its CONTEXT rail draws tabs the teacher's `when` logic cannot render, the "UNCHANGED" settings board carries five undeclared settings changes, and the roster boards use the retired word "cast" three times. None of that is the IA move — it is what the mock filled the new pane with. The LIST half plus the interaction contract can go to a build brief with the named changes in §4; the CONTENT/CONTEXT boards go back to the canvas with §2's findings as the redraw list.
