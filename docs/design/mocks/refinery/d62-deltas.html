Refinery — the three D62 deltas, drawn both ways

The shell-conformance audit (NL→schema addendum §10.5) named three places where the R3 design departs from D62's letter and explicitly refused to pick for the owner. Each is drawn here A = the design's recommendation and B = D62's letter, at the same size, with the cost of each stated. A drawing is never law — the ruling is the owner's, and whichever arm wins gets the other one banner-superseded rather than deleted.

Delta 1 · What a phone shows first

"Refinery … LIST: past refinery sessions per character (later) — defaults collapsed … CONTENT — nothing selected: pick-a-character teaching state" D62-Prefold-UX-Flow-Revamp-2026-07-05.md:446

The mechanics, verified: declaring a list forces declaring a selection (SectionWithList does not type-check otherwise), and listIsScreen = selection exists AND nothing is selected. So the choice is not a phone-layout preference — it is "does refinery have a sessions roster at all". Arm B forfeits the roster on EVERY viewport, not just the phone.
