Refinery — the empty and first-run states

Empty states are load-bearing here for a specific structural reason: under the one-shell rule, a section that declares a LIST resolves its roster as THE SCREEN on a phone when nothing is selected — so a first-run phone user's entire screen is the empty sessions roster. A bare "No sessions yet" would be the whole product on first contact. Every pane below therefore carries its own door.
