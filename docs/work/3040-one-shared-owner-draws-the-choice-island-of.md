# One shared owner draws the choice island of a turn

## What

The options a turn offers as a set of buttons are drawn twice by hand as a toned Card: once in the chat message's choices block and once in the game's choice echo. One client-shared owner draws the island, its heading and its option buttons, and both sites read it.

## Why

Two hand copies of one shape drift apart, and the notice cohort keeps them only by a ruling that names each file. A shared owner gives the island one look and lets the census hold it as its own cohort.

## Done when

One client-shared component owns the choice island, the message choices block and the game choice echo both use it, the hand-built Cards there are gone, and the notice cohort's kept rulings for those two files are removed.

## Evidence

Filled at landing: what ran and where its output is.
