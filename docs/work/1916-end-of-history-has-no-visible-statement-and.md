# End of history has no visible statement and the composer edge is below contrast floor

## What

After the last page the pager stays labelled Earlier messages and greyed, with the end stated only to assistive tech. The composer edge measures under 3:1 in every theme.

## Why

Sighted readers cannot tell history ended, and a control edge under the floor breaks the form-control edge rule.

## Done when

The end of history reads on screen, and the composer edge clears 3:1 on seeds and carried palettes.

## Evidence

Filled at landing: what ran and where its output is.
