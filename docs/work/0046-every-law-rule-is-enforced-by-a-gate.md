# Every law rule is enforced by a gate or test, or deleted

## What

Apply writing rule 9 to law: every rule in `docs/law/` is checkable or carries a reason. For each rule, name the gate or test that enforces it, add one, or delete the rule. Work one law doc at a time.

## Why

The repo keeps growing rules that nothing enforces. Prose that no check backs drifts, and cold agents follow it anyway. Enforced beats written.

## Done when

Each law doc names the enforcer of each rule it keeps, and rules with no enforcer and no reason are gone.

## Evidence

Filled at landing: what ran and where its output is.
