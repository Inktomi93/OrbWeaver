# No ruling says what a caught error from a frame document may do

## What

The blind-catch work found catch sites around interactive card and reply frame documents with no law line to judge them by: which frame failures are contained silently, which are reported, and to whom.

## Why

Without the ruling each site decides alone, and a contained failure can hide a broken card from its author.

## Done when

An ADR or law line states the containment rule for frame document failures, and each such catch site cites it or is changed to follow it.

## Evidence

Filled at landing: what ran and where its output is.
