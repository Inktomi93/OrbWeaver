# Resolve hosted instrument startup refusals

## What

Identify the shared cause of hosted instrument refusals and classify remaining failed proofs against their actual runtime evidence.

## Why

An absent instrument fact does not identify the startup operation that failed.

## Done when

Native controls establish the shared cause; affected proofs retain meaningful outcomes and remaining failures have precise evidence.

## Evidence

Filled at landing: what ran and where its output is.
