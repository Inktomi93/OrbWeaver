# Dismissed pager failure keeps its text at the pager's home

## What

Dismiss hides the failure line only inside the pinned box, so at the top of the list a mouse reader sees the text stay and the Dismiss button vanish. Either a dismissed failure leaves the pager, or the control gets another name.

## Why

The word promises the line goes away.

## Done when

The owner has ruled, and the control does what its name says.

## Evidence

Filled at landing: what ran and where its output is.
