# The per-rule entry cap refuses a confirmed analysis lore card, proven

## What

The confirm of an analysis lore card re-checks the per-rule entry cap in a book through the shared lore write. The new confirm case proves the attach gate refusal only. No case fills a book to the cap and confirms a card.

## Why

A confirmed card that skips the cap lets a rule overfill a book that the direct branch would refuse.

## Done when

A server case through the composed root fills a book with the rule's own entries to the cap, confirms a lore card for new titles and shows the confirm refused with the book unchanged and the watermark unmoved.

## Evidence

Filled at landing: what ran and where its output is.
