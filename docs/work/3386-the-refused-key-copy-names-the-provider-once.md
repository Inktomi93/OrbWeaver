# The refused-key copy names the provider once and allows for a typo

## What

The notice says LiteRouter refused this key. Get a new key; the submit failure repeats it as the provider refused this key.

## Why

A typo is the likelier cause, and two wordings for one fact read as two problems.

## Done when

One statement names the provider, offers check the key or get a new one, and the submit failure points to it.

Filled at landing: what ran and where its output is.
