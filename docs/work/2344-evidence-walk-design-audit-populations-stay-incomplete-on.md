# Evidence walk: design audit populations stay incomplete on quiet state and backdrops

## What

Across the three evidence runs (surface-Nn5cKU, surface-q297LL, surface-2sWzKu) the design audit reports incomplete populations: quiet-state unmatchedOn, unmatchedOff and unresolved on switch roots, checkbox roots, setting rows and the theme collection; contrast, gray-on-color and border-contrast with unresolvedBackdrop or paint-layer-over-base; selection-idiom unmatchedSelected. Those subjects are withheld from judgement.

## Why

A withheld population is neither clean nor failing, so the rules it feeds cannot certify a surface.

## Done when

Each incomplete population either resolves to judged subjects through the public pixel pass or a declared state attribute, or carries a cited reason it cannot, and the evidence runs show the remaining withheld subjects by reason.

Filled at landing: what ran and where its output is.
