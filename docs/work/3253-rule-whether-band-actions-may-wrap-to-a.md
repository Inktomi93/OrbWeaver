# Rule whether band actions may wrap to a second line when worded actions do not fit

## What

A list band keeps one line: an action that does not fit is folded into the More menu or shown as its icon. The first-user review of the Presets page could not find the Import door because it was an icon and, on some widths, a menu entry. The Characters and other bands carry the same icon-only import beside the primary.

## Why

Whether a band may grow a second line to keep words on its actions decides whether every worded door is possible at the 272px list, and it is a shell-wide call.

## Done when

A ruling recorded in the shell law: bands stay one line (and the fix is room, see the primary-word item) or bands may wrap their actions, with the width at which each applies.

## Evidence

Filled at landing: what ran and where its output is.
