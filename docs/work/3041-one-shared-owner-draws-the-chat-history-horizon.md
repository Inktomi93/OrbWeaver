# One shared owner draws the chat-history horizon band of the prompt assembly

## What

The band that marks where chat history enters the prompt is drawn twice by hand: in the assembly rack's pivot band and in the assembly preview. One owner in the preset feature draws the band and both sites read it.

## Why

The same band spelled twice can show two different horizons for one preset. One owner keeps the rack and the preview in step.

## Done when

One component owns the horizon band, the pivot band and the assembly preview both use it, and no second hand copy of the band remains.

Filled at landing: what ran and where its output is.
