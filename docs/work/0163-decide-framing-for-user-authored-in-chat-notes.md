# Decide framing for user-authored in-chat notes

## What

Five producers carry user-authored text that folds bare: the card author's note, persona at depth, world info at depth, after-history preset sections, and chat_injections rows. Choose between a per-origin frame slot applied at the producer and sending the text as the author wrote it.

## Why

Framing at the source would rewrite user content. Leaving it bare can make a note read as the player's words in a labelled room.

## Done when

The owner ruling is recorded and any build work is filed.

## Evidence

Filled at landing: what ran and where its output is.
