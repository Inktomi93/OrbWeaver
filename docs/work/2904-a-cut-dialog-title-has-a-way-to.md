# A cut dialog title has a way to be read in full

## What

On a short landscape phone a dialog or sheet title ends in an ellipsis under the short-window rules in `docs/law/ui-platform-rulings.md`. The same law has a rule that a cut title offers a reveal. Neither the centred dialog nor the sheet has one, and the law does not say whether dialog titles are covered.

## Why

A title that is cut with no way to read it hides the name of what the dialog acts on.

## Done when

The law states whether the reveal rule covers dialog and sheet titles, and the dialog header follows the ruling with a test at a short landscape size.

## Evidence

Filled at landing: what ran and where its output is.
