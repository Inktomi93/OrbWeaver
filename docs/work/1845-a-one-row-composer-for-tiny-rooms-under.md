# A one-row composer for tiny rooms: under the 20rem step with the keyboard up, the field and Send fit

## What

The change, in a sentence.

## Why

The symptom or the ruling behind it.

## Done when

The observable bar a verifier can check.

## Evidence

What: under the 20rem step with the keyboard up, collapse the composer toolbar so the field and Send fit.
