# The SCOPING registry slot is read at its real site

## What

Three SCOPING rows stay unbuilt on the real tree while planted replicas of their mapped registry, whose row type is a conditional over the key, read correctly.

## Why

An unreproduced lost credit hides either a reader gap or a true unbuilt member.

## Done when

A scoped slot-origin read at the real sites names the cause, and the rows are credited with a planted case or confirmed unbuilt with the reason.
