# Run retained Snap and design audits over the shared surface matrix

## What

Feed the same qualified surface, subject and layout population to existing Snap, design and runtime analyzers. Inventory their current observations, score meanings and capability requirements before sharing collectors or verdict logic. Reuse the retained artifact readers and writers. Keep this wishlist separate from the active enforcement work.

## Why

A clean score over a partial, warmed or unsupported population is not product qualification. Current accumulated layout-shift sums are not interchangeable with official session-window CLS.

## Done when

Report each eligible cell's actual scores, raw attribution, completeness, apparatus and qualification. Keep development-bridge or CDP-only observations distinct from production E2E and WebKit evidence. Preserve raw shifts when classifying virtualized activity. Add positive, planted-defect and missing-apparatus controls. Identify worst cells and exact gaps instead of averaging unsupported results into a pass. Add no duplicate metric engine, separate surface roster, screenshot baseline, private-data publication or unrelated performance-tuning campaign.

## Evidence

The [ramp enforcement closeout](../reviews/ramp-enforcement-closeout.md) records a concrete qualification gap: the dead-CSS detector flags CodeMirror's Unicode class selectors and its vendor identity marker even though CSSOM and installed vendor source establish their roles. Qualify those cases before using that detector as a matrix verdict. Preserve the raw failing runs and distinguish instrument false positives from action failures and actual product defects; do not suppress real findings or convert unavailable observations into passing scores.
