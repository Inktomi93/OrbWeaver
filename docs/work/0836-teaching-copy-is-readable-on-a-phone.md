# Teaching copy is readable on a phone

## What

Teaching paragraphs in the Settings header block and the Model roles glosses render at 10.5px on a phone (config-copy.ts consumers). Source: ~/Archives/orbweaver-reviews/phone-pass-oct10.md P2 item 2.

## Why

Sentences in the micro voice are unreadable for low-vision readers on phones.

## Done when

Teaching copy paragraphs use at least the body-small step on coarse pointers; labels keep the micro voice.

## Evidence

Filled at landing: what ran and where its output is.
