# A failed summon on a phone is visible while the Members sheet is open

## What

Suspected in review: on a phone the Members sheet covers the composer, so a failed summon shows only on the hidden composer line.

## Why

A failure the reader cannot see reads as nothing happening.

## Done when

Reproduced or refuted on a phone; if real, the failure is visible with the sheet open, with a platform CT.

## Evidence

Filled at landing: what ran and where its output is.
