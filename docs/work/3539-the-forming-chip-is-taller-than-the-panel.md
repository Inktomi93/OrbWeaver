# The forming chip is taller than the panel it stands for

## What

The forming chip that stands in for a framed reply is taller than the settled frame, so the row shrinks when the frame draws.

## Why

The row jumps at the end of a styled reply.

## Done when

The row's height does not drop when the frame replaces the chip, or the change is ruled acceptable with the measurement recorded.

## Evidence

Filled at landing: what ran and where its output is.
