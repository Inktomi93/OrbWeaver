# One kebab on the phone character sheet

## What

All Character tabs and Character actions sit side by side as two kebabs on the phone character sheet.

## Why

Two identical controls side by side give no cue which does what.

## Done when

One control, or a visible difference between them, with a CT at phone width.

## Evidence

Filled at landing: what ran and where its output is.
