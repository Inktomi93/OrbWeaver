# Rule how a success is told

## What

The feedback law states that a failure shows a toast or inline error and never neither, but it states no convention for success, so the feedback cohort cannot judge its success column.

## Why

Without a ruling, success feedback spreads per feature and the census has no bar to hold it to.

## Done when

A ruling in the client state and gates law names how a success is told, or states it is free, and the census judges the column by it.

## Evidence

Filled at landing: what ran and where its output is.
