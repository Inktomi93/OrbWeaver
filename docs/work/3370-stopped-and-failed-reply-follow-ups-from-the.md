# Stopped and failed reply follow-ups from the Stop-keeps review

## What

Six findings. A reply kept by a Stop that arrived after the last token stores no stopped mark. After a lost lease the remaining speakers' owed replies are dropped without notice. The impersonate failure toast prints the host's provider sentence to any viewer. The error branch that the user-stop check guards has no test. The kept-reply flag is never cleared after the success commit. Another member's message arriving is taken as this send having landed.

## Why

Each is a small wrong reading or an untested branch on the path a stopped or failed reply takes.

## Done when

Each finding is fixed with a case, or closed with a recorded reason; the impersonate toast shows a member no host detail.

## Evidence

Filled at landing: what ran and where its output is.
