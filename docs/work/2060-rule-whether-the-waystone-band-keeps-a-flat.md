# Rule whether the Waystone band keeps a flat type ladder

## What

Location, date, state, badge and captions in the RPG Waystone band all render at the same small type step and weight.

## Why

A flat ladder may hide which line matters most.

## Done when

Ruling: the flat ladder is not intended; the location line takes the label voice, as every other head band steps its primary line above its secondary lines and the Scene tab prints the same location at label. The location line renders at the label voice in foreground ink with date and state unchanged. A component test on the production band measures the band's share of the pane in the set and unset cases of D119a, including the top font scale at the small desktop window, and fails outside the budget; at that corner the fix takes the smallest move that fits, a one-line clamp first, then moving the date line into the body. The comment above the location line states the voices it uses. D119a records the location step and its measured cost, and D119 cites the file that holds the budget test.

## Evidence

Filled at landing: what ran and where its output is.
