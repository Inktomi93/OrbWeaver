# Decide whether the preset list row shows the preset's kind

## What

Every preset carries the kind `generation`, and the list row subtitle is the kind's only reader. Decide whether the subtitle shows it at all.

## Why

A label every row shares tells the owner nothing.

## Done when

A recorded ruling; the subtitle shows the ruled text.

## Evidence

Filled at landing: what ran and where its output is.
