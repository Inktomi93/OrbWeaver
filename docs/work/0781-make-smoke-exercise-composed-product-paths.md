# Make smoke exercise composed product paths

## What

Promote useful deterministic journeys into smoke and prove real send, streaming, cancellation, persistence, reload and passive synchronization through the application.

## Why

Boot and seeded reads do not qualify the shipped product. Deterministic external provider fixtures can prove composed paths without model spend.

## Done when

Native smoke selection includes meaningful write and conversation paths. Exact content and identity survive reload. Stop cancels the provider request before cleanup. No paid or live-model dependency is admitted. Preserve target guards and measure the revised isolated suite.

## Evidence

Filled at landing: what ran and where its output is.
