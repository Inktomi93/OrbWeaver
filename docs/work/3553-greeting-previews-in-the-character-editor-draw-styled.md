# Greeting previews in the character editor draw styled greetings as plain text

## What

The greeting preview and the greeting studio strip a greeting's stylesheet and show its text, while the room draws the same greeting in a frame.

## Why

An author cannot see what a styled greeting will look like without opening a room.

## Done when

The previews draw a styled greeting as the room does, under the character's trust step, or state that the room is where it draws.

## Evidence

Filled at landing: what ran and where its output is.
