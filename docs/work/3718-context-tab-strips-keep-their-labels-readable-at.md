# Context tab strips keep their labels readable at phone width

## What

At 390px the context tab strips hide tab labels under their scroll arrows.

## Why

Tabs whose labels are hidden cannot be told apart on a phone.

## Done when

Every tab label is fully visible or fully scrolled into view at 390px, with a platform CT.

## Evidence

Filled at landing: what ran and where its output is.
