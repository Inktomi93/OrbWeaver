# The World Info entry editor's Priority and Fire mode row fits a phone

## What

In the World Info entry editor, the Priority and Fire mode row overflows the editor at 300 to 390 pixel widths, on main as well as the editors branch.

## Why

A control row past the edge hides a control on the phone.

## Done when

At 320 and 390 the row fits or wraps inside the editor, with a CT.

## Evidence

Filled at landing: what ran and where its output is.
