# A shared display script entry carries only the fields a member's browser reads

## What

The entry sent to members includes its update time and its run-on-edit flag. Rendering and the roster read neither.

## Why

A read across a boundary between two people carries only what the receiver uses.

## Done when

The member's entry drops both fields, or a recorded ruling keeps them.

## Evidence

Filled at landing: what ran and where its output is.
