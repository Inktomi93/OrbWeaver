# Code of Conduct

Orbweaver is a roleplay app, and people are going to write some wild stuff in it. What you roleplay on your
own install is your business. This page is about how we treat each other here: issues, pull requests,
Discussions, the wiki, and anywhere someone speaks for the project.

Short version: be decent to people. Argue with the idea, not with the person who had it.

## What we want

- Be kind and be honest. You can do both at once.
- Assume good faith until someone proves otherwise.
- Give feedback on the work, and take feedback on yours.
- Remember people show up with different languages, setups and budgets. Someone running a small local
  model on a laptop belongs here as much as anyone.
- If you screw up, own it and fix it. I get plenty of practice at this one.

## What's not OK

- Harassment, including carrying on after someone asks you to stop.
- Insults or slurs aimed at a person or a group.
- Discrimination of any kind: race, ethnicity, gender, gender identity, sexuality, disability, age,
  religion, all of it.
- Sexual comments or advances aimed at people here. Keep your roleplay in your roleplay.
- Posting someone's private information without their permission.
- Threats, or encouraging anyone to hurt someone.
- Pretending to be someone else, or making a new account to get around a ban.
- Spam and ads.

Bug reports sometimes need a piece of a chat to show the problem. Trim it to what shows the bug, and if it's
NSFW, say so at the top.

## Reporting

Email **studio@inktomi.tech**. It comes straight to me, it stays private, and I won't tell anyone who
reported it. Links or screenshots help.

Orbweaver is one dude right now, so I'm the one who reads it and the one who decides. If the problem is me,
you can report it to GitHub instead with **Report content** on the comment or profile.

## What happens

It depends on how bad it is, and I can skip steps.

1. A private warning.
2. A timeout: a temporary block from the repository.
3. A permanent ban.

Threats or posting someone's private info go straight to a ban.
