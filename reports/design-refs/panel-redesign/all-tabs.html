Why this shape

The dial ring encodes time structurally — noon at the top, midnight at the bottom, the ember marker at the current hour, so “how deep in the night are we” is a glance at an angle, not a read. The four arc colors are token mixes (track-3 day, track-2 night, primary dawn/dusk). The sky disc encodes weather — rain drifts (removed under reduced-motion), storm bolts highlight-gold, fog bands, snow drifts; the horizon silhouette with the tiny ember gable says “a place, seen from its sky” — the minimap promise.

Satellites

Ring gauges are the OSRS data-orb idiom on our RingGauge geometry: arc = value/max, value inside in mono, label + value/max beneath (text is the datum). Low pools swap to destructive red (never the sole signal — the number is right there). The wallet is deliberately a COIN DISC, not a gauge: a max-less quantity wearing an arc would be a lie of shape.

The cues row

Freshness (“as of last beat” under reliable extraction, “live” under cheap) sits with the host-only veiled count (crown gold, doorway to Status → Veiled) and lite's read-only pill. One quiet row, three honest facts.

Extensible

New orb kinds (alert level, turn timer) are additive entries in the same row; the waystone never grows siblings — one bold thing.

The color grammar that makes it cohesive

Four voices, held everywhere: ember = the game's pulse (active tab, encounter, current act, quest-bound dot); crown gold = host-only truth (Game tab, veiled ledger, reveal affordances, Preview's veiled line); info blue = the story asking YOU (choice blocks); the track ramp = data identity (each pool/meter/section keeps its color from definition to orb to bar to budget slice). A new feature picks its voice and inherits the whole system.

Trust boundaries, visible

Cards render in the sandbox seal with theme tokens injected — the mock's poster deliberately uses only token colors. The member/host split is absolute: a member's DOM carries no veiled content at all (server-side strip), so the eye is a host affordance, never a member temptation.
